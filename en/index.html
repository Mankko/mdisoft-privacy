Jamtronome Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
App: Jamtronome · Android (com.mankko.jamtronome)
Developer/operator: MDI Soft
Privacy inquiries: mdisoft.lab@gmail.com

MDI Soft ("we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of people who use Jamtronome. Jamtronome provides a metronome and nearby-device synchronization without requiring account registration or a developer-operated account server. Some profile information is stored on the device or exchanged with connected peers, and the Google AdMob advertising SDK may process user and device data. This policy explains those activities.

1. Information handled by the app

  • Optional local profile: Nickname, band name, band size, selected avatar, and a resized thumbnail of an image voluntarily selected as a profile photo. These are normally stored on your device.
  • Local settings: Tempo presets, language, volume, audio output and synchronization adjustments, and other settings necessary for the app may be stored on the device.
  • Nearby connection data: Room name, four-digit join code, host nickname and avatar, and other room-discovery, admission, and synchronization details may be exchanged with nearby devices through Nearby Connections. A thumbnail of a custom profile photo may be sent after a connection and successful admission to a room.
  • Device permissions: Depending on Android version, the app may request Bluetooth, Nearby Wi-Fi devices, and location permissions to discover and connect to peers. These permissions support nearby communication; the app has no developer-operated server feature that collects or stores a history of your precise location.
  • Advertising data: Google advertising components may process additional information as explained in Section 2.

The app does not provide a user account or upload your name, phone number, email address, performance recordings, or profile photo to an account server operated by MDI Soft. This does not exclude peer-to-peer sharing or data processed by third-party advertising SDKs.

2. Google AdMob advertising and data processing

The free version includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google components may automatically collect and share data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and support related purposes, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Android advertising ID and App Set ID) and other device/account-related identifiers;
  • App launches, interactions, taps, ad views, and similar usage events;
  • App and SDK performance and diagnostic information; and
  • Advertising consent and privacy-choice information, where applicable.

Advertising data is handled by Google and related advertising providers under their respective policies rather than being collected directly into a developer-operated Jamtronome account server. Processing may occur in other countries, including the United States. Retention and deletion practices for such data may be governed by Google's policies. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Where required by applicable law, we may request advertising consent through UMP. When the app displays Ad privacy choices in Settings, you can use it to review the available choices. You may also reset or delete your advertising ID using Android settings.

3. Purposes and recipients

Local information supports preferences, profile display, room discovery and admission, nearby connections, and synchronized metronome playback. When you choose to use Nearby features, limited room, profile, and connection information may be visible to nearby users searching for rooms or to participants in your session.

Google LLC and Google's advertising service providers may handle advertising-related data under their respective policies. Jamtronome does not operate its own account analytics backend or sell personal information.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local profile and settings: You can edit profile data in the app or clear local data using Android Settings → Apps → Jamtronome → Storage → Clear data. Depending on your Android backup and restore settings, some on-device data may be backed up or restored even after reinstalling; manage device backups separately if needed.
  • Nearby session information: Connection/session details are handled while the room is in use. We cannot remotely delete data that was already displayed or transmitted to another participant's device.
  • Advertising and consent-related data: Google's retention, access, and deletion policies apply to data processed by its SDKs.

Jamtronome does not create a user account, so no separate account-deletion process applies. Contact us with privacy-related requests using the address below.

5. Security and your controls

The app is designed to use local data and permissions only for the described features. Android, Nearby Connections, and Google SDK security features apply to relevant communications. You can edit your profile, clear app data, and manage nearby-device and location permissions through Android settings. Declining permissions may restrict multi-device functionality.

6. Children and teens

Jamtronome is not designed primarily for children. We configure advertising and privacy handling with applicable local law and Google Play/AdMob policies in mind, including any necessary age-appropriate ad restrictions and consent mechanisms.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to app features, SDKs, or applicable law. The effective date and policy content will be updated on this page.
